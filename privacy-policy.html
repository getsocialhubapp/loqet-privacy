Loqet — Privacy Policy

Effective: September 30, 2026

The short version

Loqet collects no data at all. There is no account, no cloud, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. The app makes no network calls of its own. Your photos and videos are encrypted on your device and never leave it unless you choose to export them yourself.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information — not to us, and not to any third party. Loqet's App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected, and that is literal.

Your photos and videos

Everything you put in Loqet is encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM. The encryption key is stored in the iOS Keychain, marked so it can never leave your device or be backed up to iCloud. Even the thumbnails are encrypted. We have no server and no copy of your key, so we could not read your vault even if we wanted to.

Break-in reports

If you enable break-in reports, Loqet uses the front camera to photograph failed PIN attempts. Those photos are stored only inside your encrypted vault, on your device. They are never transmitted anywhere.

Exports you create

Loqet Pro lets you export a password-encrypted backup file to a place you choose (for example, the Files app). From that moment the file is under your control, wherever you choose to store it. We never see it.

Loqet Pro purchase

Loqet Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase system and processed entirely by Apple. We do not see or store your payment information. Apple's privacy policy governs the purchase itself.

Deleting your data

Because everything lives on your device, deleting the app deletes your vault, its encryption key, and all of its contents. There is nothing on a server to delete, because there is no server.

Children's privacy

Loqet is rated 17+ and is not directed at children. We collect no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
